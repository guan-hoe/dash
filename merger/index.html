SpaceX vs Tesla merger

Barclays now expects a deal could be announced before Tesla's annual meeting (due by Dec 6), most likely as SpaceX buying Tesla with its own stock. Nothing has been proposed or filed. This page lines the two companies up on the same yardsticks to answer one question: if they combine, which side is the cheaper one, and which brings more future growth?
